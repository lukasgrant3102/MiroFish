## Key Price Drivers (March 2026)

### Geopolitical: U.S.-Iran Conflict
- **Strait of Hormuz disruption:** The critical chokepoint carries ~20% of global oil supply. Near-total closure due to Iranian threats, mine-laying vessels, and drone strikes has been described as the **largest oil supply disruption in history**—roughly twice the magnitude of the 1956–1957 Suez Crisis.
- Major Middle Eastern producers (Saudi Arabia, UAE, Kuwait, Iraq) collectively reduced output by over **6 million barrels per day** due to shipping constraints.
- The largest UAE oil refinery halted operations after a drone strike.

### Crude Oil (WTI)
- WTI crude briefly surged above **$100/barrel** and nearly **$120/barrel** in early March 2026 before retreating to ~$86/barrel by March 11.
- U.S. Energy Information Administration (EIA) revised its 2026 WTI forecast upward to **$73.61/barrel** (from $53.42), with Q2 2026 expectations around **$91/barrel**.

### Recent Gas Price Trajectory
- **11 consecutive days** of rising gas prices through March 11.
- March 3: $3.109 → March 10: $3.54 → March 12: $3.598.
- One-week jump of nearly **27 cents** (late February/early March).
- Described as the **largest three-day jump since Hurricane Katrina (2005)**.
- Prices up **21%** from a month earlier; highest level since mid-2024.

### Seasonal Factors
- Spring break demand increase.
- Transition to summer-blend gasoline production (typically raises costs).
